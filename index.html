HOW TO READ THE MAP

BORN

A blue square marks a birthplace. The bigger the square, the more artists were born there.

DIED

An orange square marks a place of death, sized the same way. Where a city has both, the orange square sits just behind the blue one.

ONE LINE, ONE ARTIST

Every line means the same thing: one artist, from birthplace to place of death. Lines bend to the left of their direction, so a move from Europe to the USA curves south and a move the other way curves north.

DARK BUNDLES

Each line is drawn faint. Where many artists share a similar route, such as Europe to New York, the lines stack up and look black. Darker means more artists, not a different kind of line.

Pick a country to colour its lines: blue for artists born there, orange for artists who died there. The names appear below the map. Point at a line or square for names, and click it to keep the details below the map. Artists who died in the town where they were born have no line, only squares. Drag to move, Ctrl + scroll or pinch to zoom.

Places are matched to GeoNames towns of over 1,000 people. Villages not listed there are shown at the nearest town. Countries follow today's borders. Data: mumok artist list, cleaned 20 November 2026. Land: Natural Earth.
